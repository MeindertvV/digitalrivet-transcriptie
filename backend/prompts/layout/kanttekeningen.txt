# heavy marginal notes: long notes beside the text, often in smaller type
LAYOUT OF THIS BOOK: the text has many long notes in the margins.
- In this book a narrow column beside the text in smaller or italic type
  IS the margin, also when it looks like a column of its own: each of its
  notes is a <note side="left|right">, never a <p>, never <p type="foot">.
  This goes before the line of STEP 2 that calls a narrow column a column.
- The notes matter as much as the text: write every note in full, from
  its first to its last line, also when it runs down the whole margin or
  continues under the text. A note is never shortened or summarised.
- Go down the left and the right margin separately; a note that runs on
  past the line it starts beside is still one <note>.
- Rule A above says where each note goes: before the sentence it stands
  beside, with at= naming that sentence.
- Notes in smaller type below the text block (a commentary, an apparatus)
  are <p type="foot">, one per note, in printed order.
