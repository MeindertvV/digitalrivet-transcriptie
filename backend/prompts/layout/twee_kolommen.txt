# two columns: running text in two columns
LAYOUT OF THIS BOOK: running text in two columns.
- Read the left column whole, top to bottom, then the right column. The
  text runs on from the bottom of the left column to the top of the
  right one: a sentence broken there just goes on, no new <p>.
- Columns numbered instead of pages (305 and 306): both in
  <page_number>, "305 / 306".
- Line numbers printed in the gutter between the columns (5, 10, 15) are
  left out. Verse and section numbers inside a column stay.
- A heading over the full width goes first; a block at full width below
  both columns comes last.
