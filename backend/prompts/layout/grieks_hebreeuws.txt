# much Greek or Hebrew: grammars, lexica, editions, polyglot passages
LAYOUT OF THIS BOOK: much of the text is Greek or Hebrew (or Syriac,
Arabic, Chaldee).
- Every Greek and Hebrew word is in its own script. Never write it in
  Latin letters, never translate it, never leave it out because it is
  hard. Accents, breathings and vowel points as printed, when you can see
  them; otherwise the bare letters.
- Hebrew runs right to left: write the letters of each Hebrew word and
  phrase in their logical order (the first letter read is the first
  written), as a Hebrew text file would hold them.
- Latin and Greek or Hebrew side by side in a list or table: one <l> per
  printed line, cells separated by " | ", in the order printed from left
  to right.
