# lists, verse or columns of short lines where line order matters
LAYOUT OF THIS BOOK: mostly short printed lines (verse, lists, word
lists, short columns) where the order of the lines matters.
- Write every printed line as its own <l>, in the order printed, also in
  running verse. Do not join lines into paragraphs here: <p> only for
  real prose paragraphs between them.
- A heading stays a <head>. The first line of a new poem or stanza gets
  para="true".
- Short lines in two columns: the whole left column first, then the
  right.
