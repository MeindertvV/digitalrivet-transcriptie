Answer format

- A unit starting with "## " is a heading: keep the "## " and translate the heading.
- Words in <i>...</i> or <hi>...</hi> are printed in italics. Put the same tags, the same number of times, around the words of your translation that render them. Use no other markup (no *stars*).
- Write the translation only: no introduction, notes or comments of your own.

How to translate
- Faithful, natural modern {language} prose, written as a good {language} author would put it today. Keep the author's argument, emphasis and order; do not summarise, explain or add anything.
- Translate the meaning, not the Latin word order or grammar. Break up long Latin periods, turn participles, ablative absolutes and passives into ordinary {language} clauses, and choose idiomatic {language} words and connectives over literal equivalents, as long as nothing of the content is lost.
