[1] <i>Essere</i>, to be (German seyn or seyen), <i>esse</i>. Perfect <i>essere stato</i>, to have been (German gewesen seyn), <i>fuisse</i>. <i>Future</i>: <i>Douer essere</i>, to be going to be, or to have to be (German seyn sollen or seyn müssen). <i>Hauere ad essere</i>, to have to be (German zuseyen haben), <i>esse vel fuisse</i>.
