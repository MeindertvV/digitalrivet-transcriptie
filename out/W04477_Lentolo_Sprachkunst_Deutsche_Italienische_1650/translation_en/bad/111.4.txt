[1] <i>Genitive. Mi ricordero di esso vel d'essa, d'essi, d'esse, I will remember of him or of her, of them (masc.), of them (fem.)</i>; I will remember of that same man or woman, of those same things.
[2] <i>Accusative. Percosse essi nemici, he struck the enemies themselves</i>; he has struck the enemies themselves.
[3] Lastly, the Italians speak thus: <i>con esso lui, con essa lei, con essi loro, for con lui, con lei, con loro, with him, with her, with them; cum illis</i>; with him, with her, with them.
[4] Likewise <i>con esso teco, for con teco, or teco (tecum)</i>, with you; <i>likewise con esso meco, for con meco, or meco, mecum</i>, with me; <i>con esso seco, for con seco, or seco, secum</i>, with himself. <i>Again, con esso noi, nobiscum</i>, with us; <i>con esse voi, vobiscum</i>, with you.
