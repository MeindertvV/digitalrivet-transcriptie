[1] There are four means, and no more within the scope of my consideration, that lead to what is desired. Either the Standard fixed in heaven by the Lord of Hosts is taken down (I mean by Reformation); or white colours are set up instead of red, on one side or the other (I mean by Composition); or all the ensigns on both sides are furled up (I mean by mutual and general Cessation); or all the colours and cornets of every battalion are still displayed (I mean by Prosecution). Without Reformation there will hardly be any Composition; without Composition little hope of Cessation; without Cessation there must and will be Prosecution, which God forbid.
