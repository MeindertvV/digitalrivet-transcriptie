[1] I have often heard divers ladies vent loud feminine complaints about the wearisome variety and costly changes of fashion, and I marvel that they themselves do not prefer a bill of redress. I would that the Essex ladies would lead the dance, for the honour of their county and persons; or rather the thrice honourable ladies of the Court, whom it best befits, who may well presume on a <i>Le Roy le veult</i> from our sober King, a <i>Les Seigneurs ont assentus</i> from our prudent peers, and the like <i>assentus</i> from our considerate, I dare not say worn-out wives, Commons; who I believe would much rather pass one such bill than pay so many tailors' bills as they are forced to pay.
