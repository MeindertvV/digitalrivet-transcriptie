[1] Eighthly, let all considerate men beware of ungrounded opinions in religion. Since I know what to fear, my timorous heart has dreaded three things: a blazing star appearing in the air; a state comet, I mean a favourite rising in a kingdom; and a new opinion spreading in religion. These are exorbitancies, a formidable word: a <i>vacuum</i> and an exorbitancy are mundane evils. Concerning novelties of opinion, I shall express my thoughts in these brief passages. First, truth is the best boon God ever gave the world. There is nothing in the world any further than truth makes it so; it is better than any created <i>Ens</i> or <i>Bonum</i>, which are but truth's twins. Secondly, the least truth of God's kingdom, in its place, upholds the whole kingdom of his truths. Take away the least <i>vericulum</i> out of the world, and it unworlds everything potentially, and may unravel the whole texture actually, unless it is preserved by an army of extraordinary power. Thirdly, the least evangelical truth is worth more than all the civil truths in the world, which are merely such. Fourthly, truth is the parent of all liberty, whether political or personal; so much untruth, so much thraldom, <i>John</i> 8.32.
