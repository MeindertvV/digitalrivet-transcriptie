[1] Most Royal Sir, I beseech you once again, with tears falling from my grey head, to cover yourself as closely as you can with the best shield of goodness you have. I have something more to say, which may perhaps trouble not you yourself but your followers, more than what has already been said. There lived in your realm and reign two whom I may well call prophets, both now in a better kingdom. One of them foretold two things concerning your Majesty, and concerning these very proceedings, long before they began; since those things are now done and past, they shall be buried in silence. The other made this prediction at about the same time.
