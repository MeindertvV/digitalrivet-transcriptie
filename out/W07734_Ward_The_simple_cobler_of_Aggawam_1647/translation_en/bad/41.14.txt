[1] The sad work now is to establish better things in their Rome, and to install better men in their room; though where and how to find those things is hard, since they have cunningly laid them so far out of the way. I fear some good men cannot see them even when they look full upon them: it is likely the Bishops carried away their eyes with them, but I fear they left their spectacles behind. I use no spectacles, yet my eyes are not sharp enough, nor my hand steady enough, to cut with such fine threads as are now spun. I am I know not what; I cannot tell what to make of myself, nor, I think, can anybody else. My trade is to find more faults than others will mend, and I am very diligent at it; yet it scarce finds me a living, though the country finds me good store of work.
