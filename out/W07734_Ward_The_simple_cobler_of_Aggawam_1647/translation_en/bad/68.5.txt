[1] I would my skill were as equal to the task as my heart is, so that I might translate Prince Rupert, for his mother the Queen of Bohemia's sake, into a second Elizabeth. Do not misunderstand me. I had him in my arms when he was younger; I wish I had him there now. If I am not mistaken, he promised then to become a good Prince, but I fear he has forgotten it. If I thought he would not be angry with me, I would pray hard to his Maker to make him a true Roundhead, a wise-hearted Palatine, and a thankful man to the English, to forgive all his sins, and at last to save his soul, notwithstanding all his God-damn-me's. Yet I may be doing him wrong; I am not certain he uses that oath, and I wish no one else did; I dare say the devils dare not. I thank God that I have lived for almost these twelve years in a colony of many thousand English, and I am held to be a very sociable man; yet I may say with consideration that in all this time I never heard more than one oath sworn, never saw one man drunk, and never heard of three women being adulteresses, so far as I can recall. If these sins are amongst us in private, the Lord heal us. I would not be understood to boast of our innocence; there is no cause for me to do so. Our hearts may be bad enough, and our lives much better. But to follow my business.
