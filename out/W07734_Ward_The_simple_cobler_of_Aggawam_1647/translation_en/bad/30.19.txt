[1] I answer: many here are diligently observing the counsel of the same prophet, Jer. 22.10: "Weep not for him that is dead, neither bemoan him; but weep for him that is gone away, and shall return no more to see his native country." We make it an article of our American creed, which a celebrated divine of England has observed on Heb. 11.9, that no man ought to forsake his own country except for an extraordinary cause, and when that cause ceases, he is bound in conscience to return if he can. We are looking to him who holds our hopes and seasons in his only wise hand.
