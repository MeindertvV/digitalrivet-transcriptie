[1] My last, though not least, fear is that God will hardly replant his Gospel in any part of Christendom in so fair an edition as is expected, until the whole field has been so ploughed and harrowed that the soil is thoroughly cleansed and fitted for new seed. Or whether he will not transplant it into some other regions, I do not know. This fear I have feared these 20 years, but on what grounds I had rather bury than broach.
