[7] 30. Letter of St Ambrose to the Emperor Valentinian, again asking and urging him not to order, at the request of the Gentiles, that their altars be restored by imperial decree, and showing that from such a decree injuries are first done to God, then to his father and brother.
[8] 32. Letter of St Ambrose to the Emperor Valentinian, refusing to contend with Auxentius the Arian before the Emperor sitting in consistory as judge, because in a matter of faith bishops should judge laymen in the church, and laymen should not judge bishops in the consistory.
