<i><i>We decree, and wish it firmly observed, that neither soldier nor servant shall dare to bring a lawsuit. But if one has quarrelled with another, neither shall shout the camp signals, nor are men to be roused from there to fight. But if a dispute has been raised, no one ought to run up armed with sword, lance, or arrows; but clothed in coat of mail, shield, and helmet, let him bring to the quarrel only a staff, with which... No one shall shout the camp signals, except when seeking his lodging. But if a soldier shouts the signal and stirs up the quarrel, all his gear shall be taken from him, and he shall be cast out of the army. If a servant does so, he shall be beaten and branded on the jaw, or his lord shall return him with all his gear.</i></i>
