[1] Nominative, the Achilles [ὁ ἀχιλλεὺς]; genitive, of the Achilles [τοῦ ἀχιλλέως]; dative, to the Achilles [τῷ ἀχιλλεῖ]; accusative, the Achilles [τὸν ἀχιλλέα], also Achilles [ἀχιλλῆ]; vocative, O Achilles [ὦ ἀχιλλεῦ]; another vocative form, O Achilles [ὦ ἀχιλλέε], with the ending in -λῆ. Dual: genitive and dative of both, of the two Achilleses [τοῖν ἀχιλλέοιν]. Plural: nominative, the Achilleses [οἱ ἀχιλλέες], also in the contracted form ending in -λεῖς; genitive, of the Achilleses [τῶν ἀχιλλέων]; dative, to the Achilleses [τοῖς ἀχιλλεῦσι]; accusative, the Achilleses [τοὺς ἀχιλλέας], also ending in -λεῖς; vocative, O Achilleses [ὦ ἀχιλλέες], also ending in -λεῖς.
