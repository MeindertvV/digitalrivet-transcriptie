[1] Tygchanō [τυγχάνω], I happen (imperfect etygchanon). Future teuxomai [τεύξομαι], from teuchō. Perfect tetyxēka [τετύχηκα], from tychō. Second aorist etychon [ἔτυχον]; perfect tetyxēka from tycheō. Sustineo: I sustain. Hypecho [ὑπέχω]: future hypexō, aorist hypeschēka, second aorist hypeschon. Promitto: I promise. Hypischneomai [ὑπισχνέομαι]: future hypischnoumai or hypischneomai, aorist hypeschomēn, second aorist hypeschomen. Subeo: I undergo. Hypodyō [ὑποδύω]: future hypodysō, aorist hypedyn, from hypodymi. Privo: I deprive. Hyposeiō [ὑποσείω]: future hyposeisō. Dormito: I doze. Hypnōttō [ὑπνώττω]: future hypnōsō. Pluit: it rains. Hyei [ὑει]: future hysō, aorist ehysen. Dico: I say. Phaskō [φάσκω], imperfect ephaskon. Fugio: I flee. Pheugō [φεύγω]: future pheuxomai, perfect pephygga, second aorist ephygon. Fero: I bear. Pherō [φέρω]: futureoisō, from oiō; perfect enenochа; first aorist ēnenka; second aorist ēnenkon, from enenkō. Praevenio: I anticipate. Phthanō [φθάνω], imperfect ephthanon: future phthasō, from phthaō; perfect ephthaka; first aorist ephthasa; second aorist ephthēn, from phthēmi; the participle phthas. Tabesco: I waste away. Phthinō [φθίνω]: future phthisō. Cruento: I stain with blood. Phoinikō and phoinizō [φοινίκω, φοινίζω]: future phoinixō. Produco: I bring forth. Phyō [φύω]: future physō; perfect pephyka; second aorist ephyn, from phymi. Gaudeo: I rejoice. Chairō [χαίρω]: future charēsomai; second aorist echarōn. Aspicio: I look at. Chaskō [χάσκω]: future chanoumai; middle; perfect kekhlēna; second aorist echanon, from chainō. Dehisco: I gape open.
