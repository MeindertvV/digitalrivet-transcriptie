[1] When this desire after <i>knowledge</i> is fired by pride (as it often is, for <i>knowledge puffeth up</i>), then it is more dangerous. When men set out with the aim of seeming superior to others, of being <i>singular</i>, of seeing more than everyone else sees, of being admired and out-talking their neighbours, what <i>adventures</i> will they not undertake? How readily do they expose themselves to any notion that may serve this end?
