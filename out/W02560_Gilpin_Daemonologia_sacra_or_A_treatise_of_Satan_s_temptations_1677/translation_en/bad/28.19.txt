[1] First, Scripture plainly asserts it: the greatest number of men (who are in an unregenerate state) are expressly called foolish, blind, ignorant, men who are in darkness, men who do not know nor consider, who perish through ignorance. Others, who in comparison with these are called children of the Light, and who <i>see with open face</i>, are nevertheless (when compared with a state of perfection) represented as being in the non-age of their knowledge, unripe and imperfect. The Apostle expresses it thus: <i>1 Cor. 13. 9. We know in part, and we prophesy in part.</i> In explaining this, he compares our attainments in this world to the understanding, thoughts and speech of children (v. 11), and concludes (v. 12) that all our knowledge gives us only a dark and imperfect reflection of things: <i>we see through a glass darkly.</i>
