[1] 3. Nor are these motions <i>sudden</i> and <i>transient</i> glances, which perish as soon as they arise. (It is very common for Satan to cast motions into the heart for the sake of testing, without pursuing them further.) In this case he presses on with frequent <i>repetitions</i>, following hard after them, so that the fear grows. Men may be troubled for a long time by these messengers of Satan sent to buffet them. Though they pray earnestly that the motions may be removed, they find that they continue upon them. This must needs be a <i>hateful annoyance</i> to an upright heart, which <i>knows</i> that it is only Satan's design to frighten. Much more must it trouble those who <i>do not perceive</i> the author and purpose of such motions, but judge them to be the natural workings of their own evil hearts.
