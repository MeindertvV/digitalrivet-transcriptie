[1] 2. They are also very <i>credulous</i>. When fear is up, any suggestion takes hold. As suspicious <i>incredulity</i> is an effect of <i>joy</i> (the disciples, at first hearing that Christ was risen, <i>for joy believed it not</i>), so suspicious <i>credulity</i> is the effect of <i>fear</i>. We shall observe several things in the servants of God that show a strange inclination, as it were a <i>natural aptitude</i>, to believe the evil about their spiritual estate that Satan suggests to them. (1) There is a great forwardness and <i>precipitancy</i> in the heart to close with evil thoughts raised up in us. When jealousies of God's love are injected, there is a violent <i>hastiness</i> to entertain a belief of them at once, with all calm deliberation laid aside. This is noted more than once in the Psalms. David acknowledges this hasty humour: <i>I said in my haste</i>, Psal. 31. 22, and Psal. 116. 11. This hasty readiness to decide against ourselves, without due examination, Asaph calls a great weakness: <i>This is my infirmity</i>, Psal. 77. 10. (2) Those who are under spiritual troubles show a great kind of <i>delight</i> (if I may so call it) in hearing <i>threatnings</i> rather than promises, and in discourses that set forth the misery of the natural state rather than those that speak of the happiness of the converted. These things, in their apprehension, are more suitable to their condition and more needful for them, in order to a <i>greater</i> measure of humiliation, which they suppose to be necessary. Yet in this way they only add fuel to the flame. (3) They have an aptitude to <i>hide themselves</i> from comfort, and with a wonderful nimbleness of wit and reasoning they evade and answer any argument brought for their comfort, as if they had volunteered in Satan's service to fight against themselves. (4) Their understanding is so greatly <i>blasted</i> that Satan's <i>tempting</i> them to doubt their good estate is in itself a sufficient <i>reason</i> for them to doubt it, and that is ground enough for them to <i>deny</i> it, because Satan <i>questions</i> it.
