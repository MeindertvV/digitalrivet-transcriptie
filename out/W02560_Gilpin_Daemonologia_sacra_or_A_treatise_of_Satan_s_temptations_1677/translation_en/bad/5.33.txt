[1] Secondly, let us next look into the reasons which so strongly move Satan to these endeavours of raising up errors. If we set these before us, it will not only confirm us in our belief that this is one of his main employments (for if error yields him so many advantages for the ruin of men and the dishonour of God, there can be no doubt of his readiness to promote it). This may also serve to remind us who it is that is at work <i>behind the curtain</i> when we see such things acted upon the stage, and so may beget a cautious suspicion in our minds against his proceedings. The reasons are such as these.
