[1] 3. <i>Let none be afraid of this</i> Goliah, let no Man's heart faint because of him. A fear of <i>caution</i> and diligence to avoid his Snares, is a necessary Duty, <i>(Be sober, be vigilant, because your adversary the Devil, &c.)</i> but a <i>discouraging</i> distrustful Fear, is a dishonourable <i>reflection</i> upon God's Power and Promises to help us, and upon the Captain of our Salvation, who goeth out before us: Let us hold on in the practice of Holiness, and not be afraid. The God of Peace shall tread down Satan under our feet shortly. <i>Amen.</i>
