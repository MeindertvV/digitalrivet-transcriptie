[1] Next to Satan's deceits in tempting men to sin and to neglect their duty, his design of corrupting the minds of men by error calls for our investigation; and indeed this is one of his principal endeavours, which takes up a considerable part of his time and diligence. He is called in Scripture not only an <i>unclean spirit</i> but also a <i>lying spirit</i>, and none of these cursed qualities lie idle in him. As we may easily infer from his uncleanness that he attempts to defile the will and affections with lust, so from his lying we may conclude that he will certainly strive to blind the understanding with error.
