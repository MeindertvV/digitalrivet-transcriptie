[1] But let us turn aside a little to observe Satan's cunning in this pretence of miracles; let things be soberly weighed, and we may see enough of the cheat. This great boast is (as <i>Austin</i> has it) resolved into one of these <i>two</i>: either the figments of <i>lying men</i>, or the craft of <i>deceitful spirits</i>.
