Persons of eminent and singular holiness may be (and often are) troubled with atheistical thoughts, and have sad conflicts about them, Satan labouring (where he cannot prevail to a positive entertainment of atheism) at least to <i>disquiet</i> their minds by haunting them with his injections, if not to <i>weaken</i> their assent to these fundamental truths, in which he sometimes so prevails that good men have publicly professed that they have found it a harder matter to believe that there is a God than most do imagine.
