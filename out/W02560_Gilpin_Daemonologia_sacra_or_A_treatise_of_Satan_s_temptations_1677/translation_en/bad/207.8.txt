As the same graces exist in different degrees in different persons, and these different degrees have suitable operations, some acts being stronger, some weaker, some more perfected and ripened, others more imperfect and immature: so when Satan comes to describe grace, he sets it forth in its <i>highest excellencies</i> and most glorious attainments. You shall never observe him speak of graces at their <i>lowest pitch</i> (except where he is carrying on a design for <i>presumption</i>, and then he tells men that any <i>wishing</i> or <i>woulding</i> is grace, and every formal [<i>Lord forgive me</i>] is true repentance). On the contrary, he offers the highest reach of it, that any saint on earth ever arrived at, as <i>essentially</i> necessary to constitute its <i>being</i>, and tells them that if they do not have <i>that</i>, they have <i>nothing</i>. Let us see this in the particulars.
