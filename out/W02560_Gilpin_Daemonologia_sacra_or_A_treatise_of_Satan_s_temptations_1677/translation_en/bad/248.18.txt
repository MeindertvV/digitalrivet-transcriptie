Let no one think less of the serious practice of holy strictness in religion because these spiritual distresses sometimes befall those who are conscientiously careful in the ways of God, while profane and negligent professors are strangers to such trials. These troubles are indeed very sad, but a senseless, careless state is far worse: these troubles often end very comfortably, whereas the other ends (except God make them sensible, through conviction of their sin and danger) in that real misery whose fears occasion these sorrows to God's children. And the danger of spiritual troubles is not so great as that of a hardened heart (indeed God frequently makes use of them to prevent eternal ruin): for one who goes roaring to the pit, there are thousands who go laughing to hell.
