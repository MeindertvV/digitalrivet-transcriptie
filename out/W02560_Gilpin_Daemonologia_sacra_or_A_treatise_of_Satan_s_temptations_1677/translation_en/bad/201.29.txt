[1] 1. If anyone has fallen into a <i>great sin</i>, which a child of God commits only <i>rarely</i>, then the accuser argues against him thus: <i>They that are in Christ mortify the flesh with its affections and lusts; they cast away the works of darkness; and the works of the flesh are manifest,</i> Gal. 5. 19: <i>adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness, idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations,</i> etc. <i>Because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the children of disobedience. Be not therefore partakers with them; have no fellowship with the unfruit</i>ful works of darkness,</i> Eph. 5. 6, 11. <i>But you have not put these away nor mortified them, as your present sin proves; therefore you are no child of God.</i> 2. If anyone has relapsed into the same sin more than once or twice (suppose it is not so scandalous as the former), then he pleads from this that they are <i>backsliders in heart</i>, that they have <i>broken their covenant</i> with God, and that they are <i>in bondage</i> to sin. Here he will perhaps press that text of 2 Pet. 2. 19, 20: <i>Of whom a man is overcome, of the same he is brought into bondage. The dog is returned to his vomit.</i> 3. Or if anyone by some offence has more notably gone <i>against his knowledge</i> or <i>violated</i> his conscience, then he tells them that <i>they sin wilfully, that they reject the counsel of the Lord, that they are the servants of sin</i>, for <i>to whom you yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants you are</i>, Rom. 6. 16; and that where there is grace, though they may fall, yet it is still <i>against their wills</i>, etc. 4. If he has no clear ground to press any of the former charges against them, then he argues from the <i>frequency</i> of their <i>various</i> lapses. Here he sets their sins <i>in order</i> before them and <i>rakes</i> them all together, so that he may overwhelm them with a <i>multitude</i>, since he cannot prevail by an accusation from one or two acts. And his plea here is: <i>You are nothing but sin; your thoughts are evil continually; your words are vain and unprofitable; your actions are foolish and wicked, and this in all your employments, in all your relations, at all times. What duty is there that is not neglected or defiled? What sin is there that is not in some way or other committed? etc. Can such a heart as yours be the temple of the Holy Ghost? For the temple of the Lord is holy, and his people are washed and cleansed,</i> etc.
