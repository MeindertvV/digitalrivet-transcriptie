[1] 6. Of like nature are some <i>mistaken signs</i> of true Grace, as that true Grace fears God <i>only for his Goodness</i>. And then if there be any apprehension of Divine Displeasure impressed upon the Heart, though upon the necessary occasion of miscarriage, they, through the Devil's instigation, conclude that they are <i>under a spirit of Bondage</i>, and that their supposed Grace is not <i>true</i>, or at least not <i>genuine</i>, according to that <i>disposition</i> which the <i>New Testament</i> will furnish a Man withal. It is also another mistaken sign of Grace that it directs a Man to love God <i>singly</i> for himself, without the <i>least regard</i> to his <i>own Salvation</i>; for that, they think, is but Self-love. Now when a Child of God does not see his love to God so <i>distinctly</i> that his <i>own salvation</i> is twisted with it, Satan gets advantage of him and forces him to cast away his love as adulterate and <i>selfish</i>. Like this mistake (but of a higher strain) is that of some, that where Grace is true, it is so carried forth to honour God that the Man who has it can desire that <i>God may be honoured, though he should be damned.</i> God does not put us to such questions as these, but on the supposition that this is true; the grace of most Men will be shaken by the objection that Satan will make from thence. He can and will presently put it to the mistaken: <i>Canst thou say thou art willing to go to Hell, that God may be glorified? If not, where is thy Grace?</i> From such mistakes as these he disputes against the holiness of the Children of God, and it is impossible but that he should carry the cause against those who grant these things to be true. Satan can undeniably shew them that their Hearts will not answer to such a description of a Convert, or gracious Heart, as these false Notions will make. So long then as they hold these Notions, they have no relief against Satan's Conclusions; no comfort can be administered until they be convinced that they have embraced Mistakes for Truths. And how difficult that will be in this case (where the confidence of the Notion is great, and the suspicion strong, that the defect is only in the Heart) has been determined by frequent experience already.
