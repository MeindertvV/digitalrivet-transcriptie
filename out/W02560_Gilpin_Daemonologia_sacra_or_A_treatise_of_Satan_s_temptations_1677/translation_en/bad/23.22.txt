[1] That passage of <i>Rom. 1. 28. God gave them over to a Reprobate Mind,</i> does give some account of <i>how</i> men are brought by the Devil into these false persuasions. A reprobate mind is a mind <i>injudicious</i>, a mind that has lost its power of discerning. It is plain, then, that he can so <i>besot</i> and blind the mind that it shall not be startled at things of the greatest <i>absurdity</i>, or <i>inconveniency</i>.
