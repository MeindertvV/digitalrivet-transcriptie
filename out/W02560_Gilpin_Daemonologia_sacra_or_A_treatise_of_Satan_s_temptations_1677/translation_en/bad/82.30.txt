[1] Secondly, whatever misdeeds <i>any professor</i> of the truth is guilty of, Satan takes care that they be at once charged upon <i>the whole profession.</i> If any <i>one</i> person offends, it is a matter of <i>public</i> blame; much more if any <i>company</i> or party runs into extravagances or performs <i>actions</i> strange and indefensible: those who agree with them in the <i>general name</i> of their profession (though they differ from their wild opinions and practices as far as their enemies do) shall still be reproached with <i>their</i> follies. We see this practised daily by opposing parties, just as was foretold in <i>2 Pet. 2.2.</i> False prophets seduce a great number of Christians to follow <i>their pernicious ways</i>, and because of their wild and ungodly behaviour <i>the whole way of truth was evil spoken of.</i>
