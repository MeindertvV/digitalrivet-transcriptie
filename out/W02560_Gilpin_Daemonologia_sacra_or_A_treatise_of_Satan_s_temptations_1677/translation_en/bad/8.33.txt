[1] Fourthly, error is also eminently serviceable to Satan for bringing in <i>divisions, schisms, rents, hatreds, heart-burnings, animosities, revilings, contentions, tumults, wars, and whatsoever bitter fruits, breach of love, and the malignity of hatred can possibly produce.</i> Enough of this might be seen in the Church of <i>Corinth</i>: the divisions that arose among them were caused by it, and the Apostle suspected that a great number of evils had already been produced from thence, such as <i>debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, back-bitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults</i>, 2 Cor. 12. 20. He himself was not spared ill treatment from those among them who had been turned from the simplicity of the Gospel. He takes notice of the quarrelsome objections they had raised against him: they charged him with levity, in neglecting his promise to come to them, 2 Cor. 1. 17. They called him <i>carnal</i>, one who <i>walked according to the flesh</i>, chap. 10. 2. They taunted him as a contemptible fellow, ver. 10. They undervalued his ministry, which occasioned (not without great apology) a commendation of himself; nay, they seemed to call him a false apostle, and were so bold as to challenge him for <i>a proof of Christ speaking in him</i>, 2 Cor. 13. 3.
