[1] These men <i>let out themselves</i>, and all their abilities, to the Devil, and he knows how to make use of them to bring about the delusion and deception of others. Many ages have given examples of such men. The seducers mentioned in the New Testament were (some of them) of <i>this rank</i>, and so were called <i>deceitful workers</i>. They were not <i>really</i> under the persuasions they tried to fix upon others, but by <i>design</i> they <i>transformed themselves into the apostles of Christ</i>; they were men <i>who served not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own bellies</i>, and yet <i>by good words and fair speeches deceived the hearts of the simple</i>, Rom. 16. 18. They were men who, <i>through covetousness, with feigned words, made merchandise of</i> men, 2 Pet. 2. 3. <i>Balaam was such, and the woman Jezebel who called herself a prophetess</i>, Rev. 2. 20. Such was the Archbishop of Spalato, who for advantage could at pleasure take up and lay down his religion. Such a one was the <i>false Jew</i> (not so long ago) discovered in this place, who, being a Romish emissary, pretended to be a <i>Jew converted</i>, and, seeking a pure church under that <i>visor</i>, meant to overthrow, <i>(by private insinuations,)</i> the faith of the simple and uncautious admirers. By such instruments Satan works wherever he has opportunity.
