[1] 6. Satan makes use of <i>rewards</i> or <i>punishments</i>: on the one hand to <i>bribe</i>, or on the other to <i>force</i> the <i>affections</i>. Once these are strongly possessed, they easily prevail upon the understanding to give sentence accordingly. Men are soon persuaded to take that for truth which they see will be <i>advantageous</i> to them. Some men indeed take up with a profession of the truth which their hearts do not really approve; but the advantages they gain by their profession silence their dissatisfaction. These are said to use the profession of the truth <i>as a cloak for covetousness</i> (2 Thess. 2. 5). But others go further and are truly brought to an <i>approval</i> of that doctrine or way which makes most for their <i>profit</i>, their minds being really corrupted by a self-seeking principle. They persuade themselves (wherever there is any contest about doctrines) that the doctrine which is <i>gainful</i> is the <i>true</i> one, and they will accordingly dispute for it. Hence that expression in 1 Tim. 6. 5: <i>supposing that gain is godliness</i>.
