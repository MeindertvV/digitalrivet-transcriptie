[1] The surplus of the terumah: what did they do with it? They made beaten gold plates to overlay the walls of the Holy of Holies. Rabbi Ishmael says: the surplus of the crumbs goes to the summer fruit of the altar, and the surplus of the terumah to the sacred vessels. Rabbi Akiva says: the surplus of the terumah goes to the summer fruit of the altar, and the surplus of the libations to the vessels.
