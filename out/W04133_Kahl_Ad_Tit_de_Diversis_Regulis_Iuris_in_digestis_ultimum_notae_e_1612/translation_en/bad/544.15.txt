[1] <i>Viridariis</i>] In the Pandects of the Florentine manuscript it is written <i>virdaria</i> in <i>l. si cuius</i> §. <i>fructuarius de usufr.</i>, <i>l. in instrumento de instr. vel instr. leg.</i>, and <i>viridia</i>; in <i>l. quotiens</i> §. <i>servitutum de servit.</i>, <i>l. edificia de ser. urb.</i>, <i>l. impensa de verb. signif.</i> Hence some have derived <i>viridaria</i>, others <i>viridaria</i>. But these matters have nothing to do with Ulpian.
