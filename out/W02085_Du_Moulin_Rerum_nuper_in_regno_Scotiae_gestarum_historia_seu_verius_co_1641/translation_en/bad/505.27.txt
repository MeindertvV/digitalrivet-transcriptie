[1] Nothing could have come upon us less expected than that war should be decreed against us by the English royal council, and that on the strength of one man's report (brought onto the stage deliberately, so that he might play out that malicious business), and without any consent from your side. By this the Scottish enemies of ours have shown well enough how far their pride has advanced: they dare to seize for themselves a license to overthrow and trample the laws of the kingdom, and they force us, the faithful subjects of our most serene king, to wait, as though at their pleasure and leisure, until, grown in strength and power, they have brought about our ruin by land and sea, and that without any prior warning or declaration of war.
