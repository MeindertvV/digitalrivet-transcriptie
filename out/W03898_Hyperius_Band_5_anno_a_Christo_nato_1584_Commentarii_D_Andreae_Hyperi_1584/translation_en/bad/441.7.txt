[1] For, first, it is not said that Christ waits as one anxious or in doubt, as we say of ourselves when we wait, in which case the saying of Proverbs 13 holds true: "hope deferred makes the heart sick." Rather, he is said to wait because that glory of Christ is to be made manifest not now but in its own time; and because this is to happen in the future, for that reason Christ is said to be waiting.
