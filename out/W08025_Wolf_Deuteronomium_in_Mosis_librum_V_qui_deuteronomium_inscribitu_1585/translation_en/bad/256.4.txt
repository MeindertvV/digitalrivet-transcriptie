[1] This is the form of expression: it speaks of lying in wait, rising up, and striking. By the word "lying in wait" it denotes the attempt and intention of a planned evil, <i>to do harm to someone</i>. Whether you do this yourself or arrange for it to be done through another makes no difference, since you are the author of the deed and are held to be so. Thus Jezebel, though she had taken on the role neither of accuser, nor of witness, nor of judge, nonetheless put Naboth to death by the work of all three. Examples of this kind occur everywhere.
