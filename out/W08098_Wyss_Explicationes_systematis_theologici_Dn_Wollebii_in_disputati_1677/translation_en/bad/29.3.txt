[1] Peace to the wise man, praised in the writing of the Lord. May he be kept by the Lord day and night in the reading of Scripture. Your goodness and your spirit, the quiet breath of the people, belong to the wise man who is from the Lord; in the writing of the Lord your ... is made known in the light of the day. For I am troubled in my soul on account of the wise man; may the honour be reckoned to your good.
