[1] The first Venetian Republic is said to have had a consular administration, and then a tribunician one, for 253 years. Afterwards, in the year 697, it came under the rule of a Doge. The Duke, commonly called the Doge, is the head of the Republic and the Prince of the Senate, and he may do nothing in peace or war without the Senate's order. He consults the Senate, and the Senate decides on the matter, though the decrees are published in the Duke's name. A fixed salary is paid to the Duke from public funds, and when he dies another man is chosen by vote from among the senators to take his place at once. The Senate, commonly called the Gran Consiglio, is the foundation of the whole state. Its members are all the nobles over twenty-five years of age. They have remarkable freedom in speaking, the highest integrity in deliberating, an outstanding regard for what is right and true, an incredible zeal for preserving the liberty and enlarging the empire, and notable steadfastness in adversity.
