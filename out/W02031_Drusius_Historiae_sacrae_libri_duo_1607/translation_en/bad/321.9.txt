[1] <i>Altariumque] The word "altarium" is formed as "exemplarium" is, or "lupanarium" in civil law. Drusius uses the same word in his Dialogue 1, segment 2, as do Paulinus, Jerome and Hilary. Prudentius too has said "altar". Altar, altare, altarium; as cochlear, cochleare, cochlearium; laquear, laqueare, laquearium.</i>
