[1] 5. What if a feudum is found among immovables? Is the feudal matter to be governed by the statute of the place where the thing lies, by the statute of the feudal court, or by the statute of the thing from which the feudum depends? Answer: although the nature of a feudum looks to its source, so that the statute of the thing from which it is held seems to be the one to consider, nevertheless, since the incidents of the feudum must be regulated according to the custom of the place where it lies, and a statute cannot affect a thing lying outside the territory of the one who enacted it, the statute of the place where the feudum is found must also be considered.
