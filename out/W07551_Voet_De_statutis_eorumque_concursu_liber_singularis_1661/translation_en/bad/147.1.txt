[1] Here I shall not distinguish, since the law does not distinguish, whether a statute extends directly to goods situated outside the territory of the enacting authority, or indirectly, openly or by consequence. Things that are directly prohibited may not be permitted indirectly, in fraud of the law or of the statute.
