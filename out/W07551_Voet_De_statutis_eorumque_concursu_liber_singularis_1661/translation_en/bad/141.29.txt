[1] Hence I hold that those statutes are mixed which, although they may perhaps speak either of a thing or of a person, do not principally dispose of the thing or of the person, but prescribe the mode or formality to be observed in all transactions and cases, whether judicial or extrajudicial. And so I think I have found a plain path, one that never misleads, concerning mixed statutes, although in the explanation of personal statutes, with regard to their effects, there is no certain rule; as will be clear from what is said below. <i>Dissent. ex parte Burgund. d. tract. 1. n. 1. 2. 3. 4.
