Quaest.</i>) when the Prince, showing more restraint, although he could decide the matter by his own authority, of his own accord refers it to the Senate, so that what pleased him might be confirmed by a senatus consultum. Hence many orations of the Princes are also cited in the law, those of Severus, Marcus, Antoninus and Hadrian, which are sometimes also called senatus consulta. <i>l. 8.
