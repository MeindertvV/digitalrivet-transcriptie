But conversely, if a person is incapable in his own territory (as among us, under the declaration of the Estates of Utrecht of 14 April 1659, a woman under 16 and a man under 18), can he make a will effectively elsewhere, where he would be deemed capable under civil law, that is, a woman at age 12 and a man at age 14?
