[1] Nevertheless, by custom (though not Saxon custom), remissions are almost everywhere in Christendom admitted only out of courtesy. In that case the remitting magistrate must be protected by letters of return, so that the act does not prejudice the jurisdiction of the remitting authority. This is also the accepted rule in our United Provinces. For the confederated Provinces do not obey one supreme authority. And although they are united, they are not united as subordinate parts, so that each Province is still governed by its own laws and rights, and must be regarded as if all were separate.
