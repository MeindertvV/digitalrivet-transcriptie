On the close of a banquet and its kinds. 345 COMMON ETHICS, whose parts are: the use of speech, where the formation of words is considered, weighing the nature of things: whether they are known, fitting, not at odds with propriety and honour, useful to the persons with whom one is to speak, etc. Of words, namely which civil and pleasing words are to be used. Delivery, whose requirements are that it be timely as regards the listener, not about unwelcome, contrary or foreign matters; the speaker should not think about things... hatred, suspicion of talkativeness, tyranny, harshness. A becoming tone, where there are general and special precepts. Action, or gesture and bearing, see no. 1. Proper, see no. 2. Gesture or bearing: innate, of the speaking person, as regards the whole person or its parts; of the listener, silent, in the individual parts of the body, in the whole body, in its movement, dress, fitting, decorous, timely; adopted, which is customary for each people in mourning, rejoicing, working, etc.; arbitrary, probable, monstrous. PROPER ETHICS is conversation suited to each person's own station. It is of unequals, whose first requirement concerns the more and less dignified persons, their mutual affection; the second concerns the duty of unequal lesser and greater persons, in speech and gesture, general and particular. Of equals. Suited to a social meal: where the whole is distributed among the principal persons: host, guest, attendants; by mental communication of food, which precedes and follows; of speech, and of bearing and gesture of each kind.
