[1] A similar calumny is that we teach people to judge concerning election from the prior cause. Either they maliciously misrepresent our opinion, or they understand neither us nor themselves. We judge from the effect, that is, from faith and repentance, concerning the cause, that is, concerning election. This is to judge a posteriori. That no one should be decided upon before the end of life, whether he will be blessed: if you understand this of others, you speak rightly; but if of ourselves, or of each person's own conscience and certainty about himself, that is an impious, diabolical, blasphemous saying, overturning the whole foundation of salvation. <bl> <i>Wer</i> </bl> <i>euch das gelehrt hat, der hat euch gelehrt</i> <bl> <i>Wie</i> </bl> <i>ein Teuffel,</i> <bl> <i>Vnd</i> </bl> <i>wer euch gleich ein Engel vom Himmel</i> <bl> <i>were</i> </bl> <i>.</i> Indeed, what else should I tell you, except that if before the end of life you are not certain whether you are an heir of eternal life, you will not be one after this life: <i>Dafür euch Gott behüte.</i> For faith itself is this certainty, which is the beginning of eternal life, which all who will have it after this life ought to have in this life.
