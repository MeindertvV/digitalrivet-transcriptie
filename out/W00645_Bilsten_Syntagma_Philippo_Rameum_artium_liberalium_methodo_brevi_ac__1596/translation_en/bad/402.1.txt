[1] I prove the example with two divisors as follows. I cast 9 out of the quotient, and write the 2 that remain at the right side of the letter X. I then join the divisors by addition, saying: 1 and 2 are 3; three times two, or twice three, is 6; to this I add the remainder, namely 3, and they make 9. I write 0 above the X. Then I cast 9 out of the dividend, and 0 remains, which I place under the X. But one should not put too much trust in this rule, since, as some have found, it often fails.
